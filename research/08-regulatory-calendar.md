# 08 — US regulatory calendar 2025–2028 relevant to SMB SaaS

Regulation is the best friend of a solo B2B SaaS founder: it creates a deadline, a defined buyer, and a willingness to pay that no marketing can manufacture. In the US the picture has two features Europe lacks. Enforcement is often privatised (Prop 65 notices, ADA web suits, TCPA class actions), so urgency comes from plaintiffs rather than regulators. And deadlines move: in 2025–26 the click-to-cancel rule was vacated, the CMMC certification phase was suspended, the HIPAA Security Rule slipped to 2027 and the ADA Title II web dates moved a year. Anchor a product on a duty that already exists (a risk analysis, a written plan, a warning, a certificate, a record) rather than on a date. Dates below were verified against the sources in section 3 on 8 September 2026 unless marked *(unverified)*.

## 4. EU items that reach US exporters

- **Cyber Resilience Act**: vulnerability reporting from 11 September 2026; full obligations including a mandatory SBOM on 11 December 2027; applies to software products placed on the EU market, not to pure SaaS (idea 23).
- **AI Act**: general-purpose model duties since August 2025; transparency duties for chatbots and synthetic media from 2 August 2026; high-risk obligations moved to 2 December 2027 (ideas 34, 43).
- **European Accessibility Act** in force since 28 June 2025 with a micro-enterprise exemption (idea 30); **EU Data Act** switching-charge ban from 12 January 2027 (idea 33).
