# 08 — EU regulatory calendar 2025–2027 relevant to SME SaaS

Regulation is the best friend of a solo B2B SaaS founder: it creates a deadline, a defined buyer, and a willingness to pay that no marketing can manufacture. The table lists the instruments that drive demand for ideas in this research, with dates as verified on 6 September 2026 against the sources in section 3. Items marked *(unverified)* come from secondary sources or memory and should be checked before being used in marketing.

## 1. Calendar

| Date | Instrument | Who is affected | Ideas it drives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 Dec 2023 | EU Whistleblower Directive 2019/1937: internal channels mandatory for private companies with 50–249 employees (250+ since Dec 2021) | Every private company with 50+ employees in the EU; Norway has its own Working Environment Act rules | 26 |
| 1 Feb 2024; 5 May 2025 | Google and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (SPF and DKIM, DMARC, aligned From, one-click unsubscribe, spam rate under 0.3%); Microsoft Outlook.com applies the same from May 2025, junk-foldering then rejecting | Anyone sending 5,000+ messages a day; in practice every SMB sender | 24 |
| 1 Jul 2024 | Denmark: working-time registration law (amendment to Arbejdstidsloven, Act 89 of 30 Jan 2024): objective, reliable, accessible daily time records kept five years; "self-organisers" exempt | Every Danish employer | 27 |
| 1 Jul 2024 → 1 Jan 2027 | Denmark: Bookkeeping Act digital-system phases (registered systems from Jul 2024, non-registered Jan 2025, personal businesses over DKK 300k from 2026 *(phases unverified)*); further amendments mandatory 1 Jan 2027; Nemhandel moves to opt-out; OIOUBL to Peppol BIS migration 2028–29 | Danish businesses | 31 |
| 17 Jan 2025 | DORA applies | Financial entities and their ICT providers, including small SaaS vendors selling to banks and insurers, who receive DORA contract clauses | 28, 42 |
| 1 Jan 2025; 1 Jan 2027; 1 Jan 2028 | Germany B2B e-invoicing: receive from Jan 2025; issue mandatory for turnover over €800k from Jan 2027 and for all from Jan 2028 | All German B2B businesses | 31 |
| 2 Feb 2025; 2 Aug 2025; 2 Aug 2026 | EU AI Act: prohibitions and AI literacy; general-purpose model obligations; general application including Article 50 transparency (tell people they are talking to an AI; label synthetic media) | Every SaaS embedding an LLM is a limited-risk provider or deployer from Aug 2026 | 43 and all AI-feature ideas |
| 27 Jul 2026 | AI Omnibus in force: high-risk obligations for Annex III uses (employment, education, credit) postponed to **2 Dec 2027**; Annex I product-embedded to 2 Aug 2028 | HR-screening or credit features become high-risk from Dec 2027 | 34, 49 (hiring features) |
| 14 Apr 2025 | ViDA adopted: member states may mandate domestic e-invoicing; single VAT registration and OSS extension 1 Jul 2028; intra-EU B2B digital reporting 1 Jul 2030 | Every invoicing tool | 31 |
| 14 Apr 2025 | CSRD "stop-the-clock" (Directive 2025/794): wave 2 first reports for financial years starting 1 Jan 2027, wave 3 from 2028; Omnibus I provisional agreement (Dec 2025) raises scope to 1,000+ employees and €450M turnover with a value-chain cap based on the VSME standard *(final adoption unverified)*; Commission Recommendation on VSME 30 Jul 2025 | SMEs are out of direct scope but receive supplier ESG questionnaires; VSME is the de-facto template | 29 |
| 28 Jun 2025 | European Accessibility Act (Directive 2019/882) applies; transition for existing service contracts to 28 Jun 2030 | E-commerce, banking, e-books, ticketing, telecom, software; micro-enterprises (under 10 staff and €2M) providing services are exempt, but their customers are not | 30 |
| 1 Jul 2025 | Denmark NIS2 and CER law (Act 434 of 6 May 2025) in force; Finland Apr 2025; Germany Dec 2025 (registration by 6 Mar 2026); Sweden 15 Jan 2026; Norway not in force as of early 2026, projected mid-2026 *(unverified)*; Commission proposed NIS2 amendments 20 Jan 2026 | Medium and large entities in 18 sectors; supplier questionnaires flow down to small SaaS vendors | 28 |
| 12 Sep 2025; 12 Sep 2026; 12 Jan 2027 | EU Data Act applies; design-for-access duties for connected products placed on the market after Sep 2026; cloud switching charges fully abolished from 12 Jan 2027 | Every SaaS must offer export and switching within statutory notice periods and cannot charge egress for switching after Jan 2027 | 33 |
| 1 Jan 2026 | EU Implementing Regulation 2023/564: electronic pesticide-use records mandatory (product, dose, area, EPPO crop code, BBCH stage); Norway new fertiliser-journal duty | Every professional pesticide user; Norwegian farms fertilising over 25 decares | 1, 5 |
| 1 Jan 2026 | Belgium: all B2B invoices via Peppol | Belgian VAT-registered businesses | 31 |
| 1 Feb 2026; 1 Apr 2026; 1 Jan 2027 | Poland KSeF: large taxpayers Feb 2026, all others Apr 2026, "digitally excluded" micro Jan 2027 | Polish businesses | 31 |
| 1 May 2026 | UK Renters' Rights Act: periodic tenancies, landlord database (penalties to £7,000), ombudsman | UK landlords and agents | 13, 14 |
| 6 Apr 2026; 2027; 2028 | UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: landlords and sole traders over £50k (2026), £30k (2027), £20k (2028) | UK landlords | 14 |
| 20 May 2026 | EU Short-Term Rental Regulation 2024/1028: host registration numbers, monthly platform data sharing, national penalty rules | STR hosts and platforms | 15 |
| 1 Sep 2026; 1 Sep 2027 | France: all businesses must receive e-invoices from Sep 2026; large and mid-size must issue from Sep 2026; SMEs and micro from Sep 2027 | French businesses | 31 |
| 11 Sep 2026 | EU Cyber Resilience Act: reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and serious incidents (24-hour early warning, 72-hour notification, 14-day report); Commission guidance published 27 Jul 2026 | Manufacturers of products with digital elements (software, apps, libraries); pure SaaS is outside unless it is a remote data-processing solution essential to a product | 23 |
| 9 Dec 2026 | New Product Liability Directive 2024/2853 treats software, including SaaS, as a product for items placed on the market after this date *(unverified)* | Every software vendor | All |
| 30 Dec 2026; 30 Jun 2027 | EU Deforestation Regulation: large and medium operators; micro and small operators | Timber and forest owners selling into the chain | 6 |
| 1 Jan 2027 | Norway: issuing EHF 3.0 B2B e-invoices mandatory (Storting approved 11 Jun 2026); receiving and digital bookkeeping from 1 Jan 2030; regulations due 15 Dec 2026 | Norwegian businesses above a small turnover exemption | 31, 4, 8 |
| 11 Dec 2027 | EU Cyber Resilience Act full application: conformity assessment, machine-readable SBOM, five-year support period, technical documentation; fines to €15M or 2.5% of turnover | Same as above | 23 |
| Ongoing | EU Food Information to Consumers Regulation 1169/2011 (14 allergens, nutrition declaration since Dec 2016) and Regulation 178/2002 traceability | Every food business | 40, 47 |
| Ongoing | CAP 2023–27 conditionality (1–10% payment reductions for breaches), Norway KSL (40% slaughter-price deduction outside the scheme; external audits from autumn 2026) | Farmers | 1, 3 |
