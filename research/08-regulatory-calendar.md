# 08 — EU regulatory calendar 2025–2027 relevant to SME SaaS

Regulation is the best friend of a solo B2B SaaS founder: it creates a deadline, a defined buyer, and a willingness to pay that no marketing can manufacture. The table below lists the instruments that drive demand for ideas in this research. Dates are verified against official sources in section 2; anything marked *(verify)* was still being confirmed when this was written.

## 1. Calendar

| Date | Instrument | Who is affected | Ideas it drives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 Dec 2023 | EU Whistleblower Directive 2019/1937 — deadline for 50–249 employee private companies | Every private company with 50+ employees in the EU (Norway has its own Working Environment Act rules) | 26 |
| 1 Jul 2024 | Denmark — working-time registration law (amendment to Arbejdstidsloven) | Every Danish employer, all employees except "self-organisers" | 27 |
| Feb 2024 / May 2025 | Google & Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (SPF/DKIM/DMARC); Microsoft Outlook.com equivalent from May 2025 | Anyone sending 5,000+ emails/day to those domains; in practice every SMB sender | 24 |
| 17 Jan 2025 | DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) applies | Financial entities and their ICT third-party providers, including small SaaS vendors selling to banks/insurers | 28, 42 |
| 2 Feb 2025 / 2 Aug 2025 / 2 Aug 2026 | EU AI Act — prohibited practices; GPAI obligations and governance; most high-risk obligations | Providers and deployers of AI systems; SaaS vendors embedding AI need transparency and (for some uses) risk management | All AI-feature ideas; 34 (HR use cases are high-risk) |
| 28 Jun 2025 | European Accessibility Act (Directive 2019/882) applies | E-commerce, banking, transport, e-books and their websites/apps; micro-enterprises (<10 staff and <€2m) exempt for services | 30 |
| 1 Jul 2025 | Denmark — NIS2 and CER implementing law in force *(verify)* | Essential and important entities in DK; suppliers get questionnaires | 28 |
| 12 Sep 2025 | EU Data Act applies | Connected-product makers and cloud providers; SaaS switching/egress rules | 33 (contract terms) |
| 1 Jan 2025 → 2028 | Germany — B2B e-invoicing: must be able to *receive* from Jan 2025; *issue* mandatory from Jan 2027 (>€800k turnover) and Jan 2028 (all) | All German B2B businesses | 31 |
| 1 Jan 2026 | Belgium — mandatory B2B structured e-invoicing via Peppol | All Belgian VAT-registered businesses | 31 |
| 1 Feb 2026 / 1 Apr 2026 | Poland — KSeF mandatory (large taxpayers Feb, all others Apr) | Polish businesses | 31 |
| 1 Sep 2026 / 1 Sep 2027 | France — all businesses must *receive* e-invoices from Sep 2026; large/mid must *issue* from Sep 2026, SMEs/micro from Sep 2027 | French businesses | 31 |
| 11 Sep 2026 | EU Cyber Resilience Act — vulnerability and incident reporting obligations apply | Manufacturers of products with digital elements, including software placed on the EU market | 23 |
| 11 Dec 2027 | EU Cyber Resilience Act — full application (conformity, SBOM, support period) | Same | 23 |
| 2026–2027 | CSRD wave 2 postponed by "Stop-the-clock" Omnibus (Apr 2025); scope reduced to 1,000+ employees *(verify final text)*; VSME voluntary standard for SMEs published Dec 2024 | Large companies report; their SME suppliers get ESG questionnaires | 29 |
| 1 Jul 2030 | ViDA — mandatory EU-wide digital reporting for intra-EU B2B, e-invoicing default | All EU businesses trading cross-border | 31 |
| 2026–2028 | Norway — B2B e-invoicing mandate under consultation; proposal for mandatory issuing from 2028 *(verify)* | Norwegian businesses | 31 |
| Ongoing | CAP 2023–27 conditionality and national fertiliser/nutrient rules (DK gødningsregnskab; NO gjødslingsplan) | Farmers receiving subsidies | 1, 5 |
| Ongoing | EU Food Information to Consumers Regulation 1169/2011 — 14 allergens | Every food business | 40, 47 |
